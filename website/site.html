Stock & Storage

Unrivalled stock, ready to ship

One of AVU's greatest strengths is the exceptional quality and depth of our inventory.

Direct access, in stock

As one of the world's largest buyers of fine wine, we have direct access to many of the most sought-after estates and maintain an extraordinary selection of wines in stock, available for immediate delivery.

Every bottle is carefully sourced through trusted relationships with leading producers and long-established partners, ensuring impeccable provenance, authenticity and condition.

  • SourcedThrough trusted relationships with leading producers and long-established partners.
  • StoredIn temperature-controlled warehouses.
  • CheckedWe inspect every bottle personally before it leaves us.

Our warehouses

Owned by AVU, kept in our care

Unlike many merchants who rely on sourcing wines only after an order is placed, the vast majority of our collection is physically owned and professionally stored in temperature-controlled warehouses.

About AVU

A young house, built on relationships

AVU is a young house without centuries of heritage. What makes it exceptional today are its relationships with the world's greatest producers, and the access they create.

The founder

A sommelier course at 15

Fabio Cattaneo took his first sommelier course at 15. At 18 he joined a fine wine broker, built its European market and later opened its branch in Hong Kong.

In 2016 he founded AVU in Lugano with a clear choice: only the very top, and a personal service instead of a mass online shop.
