## 1. Hard rules

- **Never mention ARVI**, anywhere: copy, alt text, captions, file names, code comments, metadata.
- **Fabio Cattaneo's story comes only from the Forbes Italia interview** (forbes.it, 9 October 2026, "Fabio Cattaneo, AVU, business globale vini lusso": https://forbes.it/2026/10/09/fabio-cattaneo-avu-business-globale-vini-lusso). Do not add facts from elsewhere, and do not name his former employer.
- **"Among the largest buyers of Mouton Rothschild, Cheval Blanc and many other top estates" is AVU's own statement**, not an endorsement by the châteaux. Never present it as a quote from them.
- **The homepage names no estates.** It says AVU is among the largest buyers of many of the world's top estates. Estate names, these two included, appear on the Producers page among many producers.
- **Producer endorsements only on the record.** A statement from an owner, CEO or estate director counts only when filmed or written and approved in writing by the estate. Until then the slot shows a clearly marked placeholder.
- **Photos are licensed** (photographer Marco Reggi). Never commit them to a public repository and never write Google Drive links or file IDs into anything public.
- **AVU does not sell online** (confirmed by AVU): no shop, prices, stock list, "in stock" labels or product pages. The action is always an enquiry, an invitation request or a visit.
- **Rare bottles are examples of rarity**, not an offer. They may no longer be available, so they carry no price, availability or enquiry button.

## 2. Who AVU is

AVU SA is a Swiss fine wine merchant based in the AVU SA Building in Melide, on Lake Lugano. It serves high-net-worth private collectors worldwide and is also looked at by suppliers, so the site must show an international private clientele.

Positioning line (approved for the first screen): **"A Swiss fine wine merchant serving private clients worldwide."**

Headline in use: **"Access to the world's greatest wines"**.

The six pillars the site is built on (from Fabio's brief):
1. Trusted producer relationships
2. Selective access to highly allocated wines
3. Provenance and authenticity
4. Rare and collectible bottles
5. Personal service
6. Worldwide delivery (homes, villas, yachts, private jets)

Because AVU is a young house (founded 2016), it leads with what makes it exceptional today: relationships and the access they create, not heritage.

UVA–AVU: AVU read backwards is UVA, the grape. A quiet philosophy thread: "The grape · The producer · The terroir · The vintage · The bottle."

## 4. Founder story (Forbes Italia only)

- Fabio Cattaneo took his first sommelier course at 15.
- At 18 he joined a fine wine broker, built its European market and later opened its branch in Hong Kong.
- In 2016 he founded AVU in Lugano with a clear choice: only the very top, and a personal service instead of a mass online shop.
- Today the AVU team counts 85 people.

## 5. Approved copy

**Unrivalled Stock, Ready to Ship**
> One of AVU's greatest strengths is the exceptional quality and depth of our inventory.
> As one of the world's largest buyers of fine wine, we have direct access to many of the most sought-after estates and maintain an extraordinary selection of wines in stock, available for immediate delivery.
> Every bottle is carefully sourced through trusted relationships with leading producers and long-established partners, ensuring impeccable provenance, authenticity and condition. From everyday icons to the rarest collector's bottles, our inventory reflects decades of expertise and an uncompromising commitment to quality.
> Unlike many merchants who rely on sourcing wines only after an order is placed, the vast majority of our collection is physically owned and professionally stored in temperature-controlled warehouses. This allows us to inspect every bottle personally and dispatch orders immediately to clients around the world.
> Whether you are looking for a single bottle to enjoy this weekend, a complete cellar, or an impossible-to-find rarity, AVU offers immediate access to one of the world's finest selections of collectible wines.

Note: "decades of expertise" refers to the team's experience; AVU itself dates from 2016. Keep it as written or confirm with AVU before shortening.

**Our Commitment**
> At AVU, we believe that exceptional service is measured not by what is easy, but by what is possible.
> We do everything we can to fulfil our clients' requests, no matter how challenging or time-sensitive they may be. Whether sourcing an exceptionally rare bottle, arranging last-minute worldwide delivery, or creating a bespoke wine experience, we are committed to finding a solution.
> One example perfectly illustrates our philosophy. A client urgently needed a rare bottle in Los Angeles the very next day. Rather than relying on conventional shipping, we put one of our account managers on the first available flight to personally hand-deliver the bottle. It wasn't the easiest solution, but it was the right one.

**Personal Wine Butler** (the private client account manager): your dedicated private wine advisor. Builds and manages the collection, says when wines are ready to drink, advises on food and wine pairings, organises private tastings and wine trips, handles special requests. Serves most clients in their own language.

**Experiences**: private dinners, tastings, producer visits and wine events.

## 6. The seven values

Show each value with its proof (a fact, a case, a picture), not as a list of adjectives.

1. **We Never Stop at "No"**: the impossible is often a challenge waiting to be solved.
2. **We Go Beyond Expectations**: attention to detail, responsiveness, dedication to every client.
3. **We Offer Only the Best**: uncompromising standards for provenance, authenticity and excellence.
4. **Relationships Come First**: trust built with the world's finest producers and with clients creates opportunities that cannot be bought.
5. **Provenance Is Everything**: impeccable provenance, perfect storage, absolute authenticity.
6. **Passion Drives Everything We Do**: travelling, discovering, learning and sharing extraordinary wines.
7. **The Client Always Comes First**: personalised advice at every stage of each collector's wine journey.
