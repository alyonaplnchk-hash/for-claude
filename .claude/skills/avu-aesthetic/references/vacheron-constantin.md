# Vacheron Constantin (vacheron-constantin.com): what we measured and what AVU takes

Measured with a headless browser at 1440 px wide in October 2026 on seven pages: Home, The Maison (270 years), Overseas collection, an Overseas product page, Les Cabinotiers, Services, Boutiques. Values are computed styles. Use them to explain choices, not to copy the site.

## 1. The system in one paragraph

A warm cream page with dark brown text, a humanist sans for headlines in sentence case, and a neutral grotesque for everything small. It feels like paper and leather rather than a gallery. Portrait-format photographs (4:5) dominate; product pages read like a catalogue entry with a specification table. Pages close with a framed invitation to visit a boutique.

Observations
- Headlines are short phrases in sentence case: "Crafting new perspectives", "From Geneva, since 1755", "A commitment to excellence", "Discover our timepieces in person".
- Labels do the categorising (MANUFACTURE, SERVICES, LES CABINOTIERS); headlines do the feeling.
- The big centred statement after the hero is set at 40 px in a 608 px column: one or two sentences about who they are.

## 4. Layout and grid

- Wide layouts (container up to 2660 px for media), text in narrow columns: 435–448 px beside images, 480–501 px in spreads, 608–662 px for centred statements.
- Header sits over the first film: centred wordmark with the Maltese cross, navigation on both sides (Watches, Stories, Boutiques, Services, Contact).
- Collection grid: 4 columns of cream tiles (246 × 347, ratio 0.71), each with a collection label, the model name and one spec line ("41.5 MM - WHITE GOLD").
- Product page: two images side by side above the fold (662 × 990 and 629 × 990), name and reference beside them, then gallery, then a "Watch specifications" table.

- 4:5 portrait is the house format for product, people and craft.
- People appear: an artisan at the bench (home hero), a client in a boutique, an ambassador portrait. Faces are fine when the moment is natural.
- Craft close-ups (dials, engraving, hands with tweezers) alternate with architecture of the Manufacture.

## 6. Buttons, links and motion

- No pill buttons. Calls to action are 12 px caps text links with 0.2 em spacing ("DISCOVER MORE", "BOOK AN APPOINTMENT", "CONTACT US"), often with a short line.
- The only boxed control is functional (Filters): a 66 px rectangle, 24 px padding, radius 0.
- Slides and panels move with 0.6 s cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1), an ease-in-out.
- The product page uses one action: "Enquire".

## 7. Page patterns

**Home.** Film of an artisan, label + H1 "Crafting new perspectives" + "Discover more" → centred statement ("Vacheron Constantin is the world's oldest watch Manufacture…") → collection carousel on cream tiles → full-width product banners → editorial spread on Métiers d'art → institutional banner → framed boutique card "Discover our timepieces in person / Find the nearest boutique" → services banner → footer.

**The Maison.** Film "From Geneva, since 1755" → quote as headline ("Do better if possible, and that is always possible.") → zigzag of portrait images and short texts → triptych of hands at work → architecture → careers banner "Play your part".

**Collection (Overseas).** Film with H1 → intro → 4-column grid with Filters → ambassador story → statement → three lifestyle portraits → framed boutique card.

**Product.** Two-up images → collection label, name, reference in caps, "Enquire" → gallery in 4:5 → limited-edition banner → "Watch specifications" table: caps label on the left, value on the right, hairline between rows → straps → boutique card.

**Les Cabinotiers (bespoke).** Two films → statement → black bands for the most complex pieces → portrait pairs → "Bespoke services" → "Annual themes" → "Craft your bespoke timepiece / Contact us".

**Services.** Image with H1 "A commitment to excellence" → statement → 2 × 2 grid of square images with white H2 and caps links (Repair and restoration, Adjust and care, Warranty and passport, Heritage documents) → concierge block "We are here to assist you" with "Book an appointment" beside a boutique photo.

**Footer.** Cream, contact and appointment links with phone, social icons, the cross emblem centred, link columns, legal row.
